# TestDrive game pages

The Waves page, the Sounds page, and the Art and Decisions pages (in Hebrew) of the TestDrive game,
online: https://torahworldwide.github.io/testdrive-pages/

This repo only holds the pages and the sounds they play. All sounds are free to use (CC0):
recordings by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) and from Freesound.org; see `sfx/` for credits.
The Art page shows pictures and clips of the game's characters, weapons and scenery; the Decisions
page shows every choice about the game that waits for its owner, each option with pictures from the
game. Those pictures and clips are kept in the repo testdrive-media (published by
`GamePages/publish_media.py`).
The data the pages edit (the waves, the sounds, the decisions and notes) lives in a private repo, and
saving needs its owner's key.
Built and published by `GamePages/publish.py` in the game's repo.
